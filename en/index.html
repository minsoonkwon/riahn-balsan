About

Near Balsan Station Exit 5,
RIAHN Hair Balsan

RIAHN Hair Balsan is a hair salon near Exit 5 of Balsan Station. Along with cuts, perms and color, we offer scalp treatments, head spa and men's grooming services.

First-time guests who book on Naver and write a review receive a discount. We care for your hair with a range of services, including root volume perms and our high-intensity lifting treatment.

Opened in 2012 with 10 styling chairs, we are in Room 306 on the 3rd floor of the Sinwoo Building, 290 Gonghang-daero, Gangseo-gu, Seoul — 84 m from Balsan Station Exit 5, in front of the bus stop, with Twosome Place on the ground floor. Free parking is available in the lot behind the building, and you are welcome to bring your pet.

Why RIAHN Hair Balsan

  • A discount on your first visit when you book on Naver and post a photo review
  • A wide range of perms and treatments, including root volume perms and high-intensity lifting treatments
  • Scalp treatments, head spa and men's grooming (barbershop) services
  • Pets welcome and free parking
