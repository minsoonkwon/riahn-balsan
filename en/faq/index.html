FAQ

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about booking, parking, hours and prices.

Home/FAQ
How do I book?
Book your date and time on Naver Booking. You can also contact us by phone (+82-2-2668-8111), Instagram DM, our blog or Facebook.
Is parking available?
Yes. Parking is free in the lot behind the building.
What are your hours?
We are open 09:00–22:00 on Mondays, Wednesdays, Thursdays, Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays.
When are you closed?
We are closed every Tuesday.
How much do services cost?
Men's cut ₩24,000–₩30,000, women's cut ₩26,000–₩35,000, bangs trim ₩7,000, basic perm and root volume perm ₩80,000, full color ₩80,000, root color (under 3 cm) ₩64,000 and hair care treatment ₩80,000.
Is there a first-visit discount?
Yes. On your first visit, book on Naver and post a photo review to receive a discount.
What if I have a treatment with a perm or color?
Our hair care treatment is 50% when you have a perm or color.
Can I book a specific designer?
Choose your designer when you book. Each designer's specialties are on the Our Designers page.
How do I find the salon?
We are in Room 306 on the 3rd floor of the Sinwoo Building, 84 m from Balsan Station Exit 5, in front of the bus stop with Twosome Place on the ground floor.
Can I bring my pet?
Yes. You are welcome to visit with your pet.

Didn't find your answer? Call us during opening hours (09:00–22:00, closed Tuesdays).
