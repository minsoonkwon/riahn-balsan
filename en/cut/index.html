From dandy cuts to layered cuts,
haircuts at Balsan

We offer men's cuts, women's cuts and bangs trims. A men's cut is ₩24,000–₩30,000, a women's cut is ₩26,000–₩35,000 and a bangs trim is ₩7,000.

Head Director Hyeok Heo and Deputy Director Jiyeon specialize in dandy and layered cuts, and Designer Minji in men's dandy cuts. If you want volume too, see perms at Balsan.

Recommended for

  • Men looking for a dandy cut
  • Anyone wanting a light, natural style with a layered cut
  • Those who just want a quick bangs trim
  • Anyone who wants a cut after work (open until 22:00)
